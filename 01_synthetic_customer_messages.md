# Step 1 — Synthetic customer messages

All 12 messages below are fictional. They are created only for this learning exercise; no real customer information is included.

| ID | Language | Customer message | Channel | Design note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M01 | English | I made a UPI payment of ₹850 to a shop, but the amount was deducted from my account and the merchant hasn't received it. Please help. | App chat | Failed payment |
| M02 | English | My refund for a cancelled transaction has not been credited even after 5 working days. Can you check its status? | Email | Refund delay |
| M03 | English | I received a notification saying that someone tried to log in to my ChillarPay account from another device. I didn't attempt any login. | App chat | Suspicious login |
| M04 | English | I am unable to complete my KYC verification. The app keeps showing an error even after uploading my documents multiple times. | Email | KYC issue |
| M05 | Hinglish | Bhai, payment successful dikha raha hai but dukaan wale ko paise nahi mile. Mere account se amount cut ho gaya hai. | App chat | Hinglish failed payment |
| M06 | Hinglish | Yaar, mera refund abhi tak nahi aaya. Transaction cancel hue ek hafta ho gaya hai. Please jaldi check karo. | Twitter | Hinglish refund |
| M07 | English | Someone called claiming to be from ChillarPay support and asked me to share my OTP to reverse a failed payment. Is this genuine? | App chat | OTP social-engineering attempt |
| M08 | English | Worst payment app ever! My money has been deducted twice for one transaction, and your support team is doing absolutely nothing. I want my money back immediately! | Twitter | Angry duplicate deduction |
| M09 | English | KYC pending since yesterday. Why is it taking so long? | App chat | Short/underspecified |
| M10 | English | I transferred ₹2,000 to a friend, but the transaction shows pending. The amount has been deducted, and I am worried because I also received a suspicious payment request from an unknown user. | Email | Mixed payment + fraud concern |
| M11 | English | I recently changed my mobile device, and now I cannot access my ChillarPay account. It keeps asking me to complete verification again. | App chat | Device/account verification |
| M12 | English | My account has been temporarily blocked after I reported an unauthorised transaction. I need access to my balance, and I haven't received any update about the disputed amount. | Email | Account block + fraud dispute |

**Language mix:** 10 English messages and 2 Hinglish messages (M05–M06).

**Tricky cases:** M07 asks whether an OTP request is genuine; M10 combines a pending transfer with an unknown payment request; M12 combines a blocked account with an unauthorised-transaction dispute.
